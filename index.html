Pause Challenge

Freeze it at the right moment.

A silhouette sits centre screen. A matching shape flies in and bounces across it. Tap to freeze the shape — you score 0 to 100 on how close the two centres were at the instant you let go.

Support Coming to iPhone

Tap now and that is a Good. Wait two frames and it is not.

Three ways to play

Classic

Fixed time · 30s

Daily Challenge

Seeded · 10 rounds

Chaos

One pass only · wrong shapes

The Daily Challenge is generated from the date, so every player gets the same ten rounds — the same shapes, entering from the same directions, at the same speeds. Chaos gives you a single pass at each shape and mixes in shapes that do not match the silhouette; freeze one of those and it costs you points.

How a tap is scored

Distance between the two centres at the moment you froze the shape, banded into five outcomes. Perfect is a genuine bullseye — at base speed the shape crosses it in about 25 milliseconds.

PERFECT100
GREAT85–99

OK20–54

Built to be quiet

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your scores and play history live on your device. Game Center handles leaderboards if you are signed in, and nothing else leaves your phone — the privacy policy is the short version of that sentence with the details filled in.
