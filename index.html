Pause Challenge

Pause it at the right moment.

An outline sits center screen. A matching shape flies in and bounces across it. Tap to freeze the shape — you score 0 to 100 on how close the two centers were at the instant you let go.

Three ways to play

Classic

Fixed time · 30s

Daily Challenge

Seeded · 10 rounds

Chaos

Single Pass · decoy shapes

The Daily Challenge is new every day and every player gets the same ten rounds — the same shapes, entering from the same directions, at the same speeds. Chaos gives you a single pass at each shape and mixes in decoy shapes that do not match the outline; freeze one of those and incur a penalty of negative points.

How a tap is scored

Normalized distance between the two centers at the moment you froze the shape, banded into five outcomes as described below.

PERFECT100
GREAT85–99
GOOD55–84
OK20–54
MISSUnder 20

Built to be quiet

No accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking. Your scores and play history live on your device. Game Center handles leaderboards if you are signed in, and nothing else leaves your phone — the privacy policy is the short version of that sentence with the details filled in.
